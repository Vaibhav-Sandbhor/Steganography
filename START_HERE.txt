❓ COMMON QUESTIONS ANSWERED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Q: "Will the image look different after encoding?"
A: No! The modified image is visually identical to the original.

Q: "What's LSB steganography?"
A: Hiding data in the least significant bit of pixels (1 bit per channel).
   Read ARCHITECTURE.md for details.

Q: "Can I use JPG instead of PNG?"
A: Not recommended. JPG compression will corrupt the hidden data.
   PNG is required.

Q: "How much can I hide?"
A: Use the capacity formula: (width × height × 3 - 32) ÷ 8 bytes

Q: "What if I forget the password?"
A: The message is encrypted. Without the correct password, it's 
   unrecoverable. Choose passwords wisely!

Q: "Can anyone detect the hidden message?"
A: Not with naked eye (visually imperceptible). But advanced
   steganalysis tools might detect the presence of data.
   This is an educational tool, not military-grade.

Q: "How long does encoding take?"
A: ~2 seconds for typical images. Larger images take longer.

Q: "Can I use other Python versions?"
A: Python 3.8+ is required. Older versions won't work.
